About

A free tool for drawing publication-quality syntax trees, for people who already know how trees work.

  • Build a tree by dragging pieces, or paste labelled brackets or LaTeX (qtree, forest).
  • Add movement arrows, triangles and traces; set fonts and sizes for labels and words.
  • Export SVG, PNG, PDF or TikZ.
  • Nothing is uploaded. Your tree is saved in your browser only, so export a file for anything you want to keep.

For teachers

Teaching trees to beginners? See the Shapes and Language game and its sandbox.
